## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution

FarmTrace: Digital Farm-to-Consumer Tracking


FarmTrace is an innovative digital platform designed to connect farmers, distributors, retailers and consumers through a transparent agricultural supply chain.Develop a digital platform to connect farmers directly with consumers and buyers. Use QR Code Technology to identify and track agricultural products from farms to consumers.Assign a unique QR code to every agricultural product batch.Store important information such as farmer details, farm location, crop name, harvesting date, product quantity and transportation details.Include digital payment and order management facilities.Provide multilingual support to make the platform accessible to rural farmers.

Key Features:

1.Allows farmers to create digital profiles and register their agricultural products.

2.Generates a separate QR code for every agricultural product batch.

3.Tracks products from harvesting to transportation, storage and final delivery.

4. Direct Farmer-to-Consumer Marketplace: Connects farmers directly with consumers, retailers, wholesalers and supermarkets.
   
5.Supports transparent transactions and payment tracking.

6. Allows consumers to scan QR codes and view available product information.

How It Addresses the Problem:

1.Connects farmers directly with buyers, reducing unnecessary intermediaries.

2. Creates opportunities to reduce additional costs caused by multiple intermediaries.
   
3. Allows consumers to check available information about the products they purchase.
   
4.Provides farmers with market price information and demand trends.

5. Monitors transportation, storage and distribution activities.

Innovation and Uniqueness

1. Every agricultural product batch receives a unique digital identity for easy identification and tracking.

2. Records the product journey from the farm to the final consumer.

3. Uses available market data to recommend suitable selling prices.

4. Maintains digital records of harvesting, transportation, storage and distribution.

5. Creates a connection between farmers and buyers without unnecessary intermediaries.

## Feasibility and Viability

1.Feasibility of the Idea

* FarmTrace is technically feasible because it can be developed using existing technologies such as GPS, QR codes, cloud computing, and mobile applications.

* Farmers can easily register their products and update information about farming, harvesting, and transportation.

* Consumers can scan QR codes to know the origin, quality, and journey of agricultural products.

* The platform can be developed using affordable technologies and smartphones.

2. Potential Challenges and Risks

*Some farmers may not know how to use digital platform.

*Poor internet access in rural areas may affect real-time data updates.

*Developing and maintaining the platform may require financial investment.

3. Strategies for Overcoming These Challenges

*Develop an easy-to-use application with local language support.

*Allow farmers to enter data offline and synchronize it when internet connectivity is available.

*Use low-cost QR codes and open-source software to reduce development expenses.
